# InjeraDAO 🍞🇪🇹🇪🇷🌍

**Break bread. Build together.**

InjeraDAO is a community bringing builders together through injera, Habesha food and East African culture. We believe some of the best conversations and collaborations start around a shared plate.

**Rule #1: Eat injera.**

🌐 **Website:** https://injera.network

## What we're building
- **Find injera:** Discover places serving Ethiopian, Eritrean and other injera-centered cuisines.
- **Gather:** Bring founders, designers, engineers, artists and curious neighbors together around food.
- **Learn:** Explore injera, regional dishes, ingredients and food traditions with cultural care.
- **Collaborate:** Turn shared meals into friendships, projects and community-led initiatives.
- **Contribute:** Help improve open food discovery data, accessible resources and community experiences.

InjeraDAO is culture-first and people-first. You do not need to work in technology, hold cryptocurrency or know anything about DAOs to share a meal.

## Terminology and editorial principles
Use **InjeraDAO** for the community brand and **injera.network** for the web destination. Emphasize Ethiopian and Eritrean cultural roots while welcoming the broader East African diaspora and people discovering this food for the first time. Explain "DAO" without overstating tokenization, governance or legal structures. Never fabricate restaurant details, events, statistics, claimed partnerships, endorsements, member counts or prices.

This repo is a **content and distribution source**, not the deployed website. Publishing into the application's frontend, generating route metadata and checking live indexing are separate integration steps.
