# Contributing to InjeraDAO public content

Thanks for helping make this a more useful and welcoming resource.

## Editorial commitments
1. **Respect roots:** Credit Ethiopian and Eritrean traditions, and avoid flattening diverse regional practices into one universal narrative.
2. **No fake listings:** Do not invent restaurant names, exact addresses, prices, hours, menus, certifications, events or partnerships.
3. **Label uncertainty:** Use "not verified" rather than inventing missing data.
4. **Record provenance:** For factual venue entries, keep source URL, observed date, evidence type, last menu verification and last price verification separately.
5. **Consent:** Do not reuse portraits, restaurant photography, menus or stories without appropriate rights or permission.
6. **No misleading SEO:** Avoid keyword stuffing, auto-generated city pages with no content, undisclosed affiliate marketing, fabricated reviews or fake event pages.
7. **No private invites:** Do not put private or invitation-only chat links into public content, previews or schema.
8. **Inclusive accessibility:** Use helpful alt text and accessible copy; don't imply someone must eat injera due to allergies or dietary restrictions.
9. **Clarify status:** Future features are proposals, not launched features. Verify routes and UI copy before publishing.
10. **Corrections:** Track correction request, source, outcome and review date.

## Suggested content PR template
- Audience and intended page
- Primary question answered
- Original sources and date checked
- Cultural/editorial review notes
- Links to existing related pages
- Metadata/schema changes
- Screenshots only when properly licensed
- Claim verification and functionality check
