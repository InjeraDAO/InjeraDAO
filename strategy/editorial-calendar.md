# 12-week editorial plan

No invented venues, dates, reviews or event partners. Publish only researched pieces, keeping food-community usefulness ahead of search volume.

| Week | Pillar page / article | Supporting content | Community prompt | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | About InjeraDAO | Explainer: DAO vs food community | What does sharing food mean to you? | Brand statements |
| 2 | What is injera? | Teff and fermentation basics | First injera memory | Food accuracy |
| 3 | Ethiopian and Eritrean food primer | Newcomer menu guide | Favorite first dish | Dish terminology |
| 4 | How to eat injera respectfully | Dining etiquette FAQ | What would you ask a host? | Multiple cultural perspectives |
| 5 | Shiro varieties | Legumes and spice basics | Best shiro pairing | Recipe diversity |
| 6 | Vegetarian dishes with injera | Misir and greens guides | Favorite meatless plate | Dietary caveats |
| 7 | Eritrean food introduction | Zigni and regional terminology | Eritrean dish recommendation | Eritrean editorial review |
| 8 | Teff: crop to flatbread | Grain shopping explainer | What grain do you use? | Agricultural citations |
| 9 | Finding injera in your city | How to verify a venue | Suggest a missing spot | Primary venue confirmation |
| 10 | Hosting an injera gathering | Accessibility and cost guide | Would you host? | Venue-first consent |
| 11 | Builders at the table | Community participant interview | What are you building? | Explicit interview consent |
| 12 | Progress and learning recap | Corrections and improved directory data | What should we improve? | Actual numbers only |

## Distribution
For each substantive guide: one short video script, one carousel outline, one single-image caption, one short-form text post, one email-ready blurb and an internal link from a related page. Do not repost near-identical copy across all channels. Preserve local spelling, pronunciation and cultural nuance.

## Editorial pipeline
Pitch → primary-source research → cultural review → fact-check → metadata → publish → link to related guide → test indexability → monitor questions → update with evidence.
