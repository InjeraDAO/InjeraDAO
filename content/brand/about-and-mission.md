# About InjeraDAO

## Break bread. Build together.

InjeraDAO is a community building connections across East Africa and beyond through a simple idea: **good things happen when people share food**.

We bring builders, founders, developers, designers, creatives, students and food lovers around the same plate. We want to make it easier to find injera, organize shared meals and meet people whose ideas and experiences might otherwise never cross paths.

Our roots are in Ethiopian and Eritrean food cultures, where injera is often both the foundation of a meal and the means of sharing it. We celebrate those roots, welcome people from every background and treat the food with the respect its traditions deserve.

**Rule #1: Eat injera.**

## Why a food-first community?

Tech events can feel transactional. A meal changes the atmosphere. We talk, listen, ask better questions, introduce new friends and make room for people who may not attend a traditional networking event.

Sharing a plate does not magically solve divisions. It does create a human place to begin.

## What does DAO mean here?

DAO stands for *decentralized autonomous organization*. In broad terms, a DAO is a way for a community to coordinate decisions and resources together. At InjeraDAO, the name expresses an aspiration toward participatory, community-led coordination—not a claim that a token, smart-contract governance system or formal legal DAO already exists. Our starting point is much simpler: **eat together, meet each other and build things worth sharing**.

## Our goals

1. Make injera and injera-serving places easier to discover.
2. Create welcoming, recurring gatherings rooted in food and culture.
3. Build bridges among Ethiopian, Eritrean and broader East African communities.
4. Help newcomers learn about injera, dishes, ingredients and respectful eating traditions.
5. Connect people with shared interests so useful collaborations can grow organically.
6. Invite contributors to build better food discovery tools and open, carefully sourced cultural resources.

## Who belongs?

Everyone who comes with curiosity and respect. You do not need to be Ethiopian or Eritrean, know how to code, own crypto or belong to an existing network.

## Join a table

Start by learning about injera, finding a nearby place that serves it, inviting a friend to a meal or proposing a community gathering at [injera.network](https://injera.network). Event dates and places are announced only when confirmed.

**One plate. More connections.**
