# InjeraDAO gatherings

## A table is a better place to start

What if networking began with a shared meal instead of a sales pitch? InjeraDAO gatherings bring builders, founders, developers, students, artists and curious people together around Ethiopian and Eritrean food.

## What to expect
A relaxed meal; introductions that feel natural; space to ask about the food; and conversations about ideas, work and life. You can come alone, bring a friend or attend simply to discover injera.

## Who is welcome?
Anyone respectful of the culture and of fellow guests. You do not need to know software, blockchain or the local tech scene.

## Want to host?
1. Identify a restaurant or venue that is comfortable accommodating a group.
2. Confirm accessibility, dietary options, seating capacity and typical per-person cost.
3. Propose a date and get venue permission before promoting it.
4. Share the expected format and whether participants pay for their own meals.
5. Publish confirmed date, local timezone, venue, address, booking requirements and price.
6. Invite guests to introduce themselves without pressuring anyone to pitch or post photographs.

## Ideas for formats
- **First Plate:** Friendly introduction to injera for newcomers.
- **Founder Fridays, with Food:** Low-pressure conversations between builders.
- **Code & Coffee Ceremony:** Technical discussions paired with regional coffee culture where appropriate.
- **Bring a Builder:** Introduce a friend from outside your usual circles.
- **Taste the Menu:** Learn from a restaurant team about the dishes they serve.
- **Local Ingredients Night:** Explore teff, spices, legumes and the people who supply them.
- **Community Demo Table:** Optional short show-and-tell, after everyone eats.

## Keep events honest
Do not post a date, restaurant, partner, ticket price, venue photo, capacity or attendance number as fact until confirmed. Ask before filming or photographing people. Respect the restaurant staff, their kitchen and their time.

For new gatherings and participation, see [injera.network](https://injera.network).
