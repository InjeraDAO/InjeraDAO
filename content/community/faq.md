# InjeraDAO questions and answers

## What is InjeraDAO?
InjeraDAO is a community bringing people together through injera, Ethiopian and Eritrean food, and builder-friendly cultural gatherings. Its website is https://injera.network.

## Is InjeraDAO a restaurant?
No. InjeraDAO is a community and food discovery initiative. Restaurants and other venues mentioned on its platform are independent unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## What is injera?
Injera is a fermented, soft flatbread central to many Ethiopian and Eritrean meals. It commonly forms the base of a platter and is used to scoop up accompanying dishes.

## What does DAO stand for?
DAO stands for decentralized autonomous organization—a concept for coordination and governance by a community. InjeraDAO's name signals a community-led ambition; it does not imply a live token, formal governance protocol or financial product.

## Do I need cryptocurrency?
No. You can attend a meal, discover food or participate in community activities without owning cryptocurrency.

## Do I need to be a developer?
No. "Builder" includes anyone creating something—software, art, a business, a community or a better neighborhood.

## Do I need to be Ethiopian or Eritrean?
No. Everyone with a respectful interest in injera and the cultures connected to it is welcome.

## Where can I find injera near me?
Search for Ethiopian or Eritrean restaurants or grocers in your city. injera.network is focused on making injera discovery easier. Confirm venue hours, menus and stock before traveling.

## Is injera gluten-free?
Pure teff is naturally gluten-free, but injera may contain blended grains such as wheat or be exposed to cross-contact. Always check directly with the restaurant or producer.

## Is Ethiopian food spicy?
Some dishes are spicy; many others are mild. Ask about individual dishes and the kitchen's spice level.

## How do I join an InjeraDAO meal?
Check injera.network for announced gatherings. Only rely on events with confirmed dates, locations and instructions.

## Can I suggest a restaurant or correct a listing?
The project welcomes community-contributed corrections and venue suggestions. Look for contribution options on injera.network as they are rolled out.

## Can my restaurant get involved?
Restaurants interested in hosting a meal or helping correct their information can contact the community through channels listed on injera.network. Do not assume a listing means formal partnership.

## Are there membership fees?
No general fee or membership program is announced in this content. Individual restaurants or future events may charge for food; check each confirmed event.

## What does "Rule #1: Eat injera" mean?
It's a playful reminder that shared food and relationships come first. It is not a requirement to eat a particular food or ignore dietary restrictions.

## How does InjeraDAO differ from a typical networking event?
It centers the gathering on a meal and cultural exchange, with professional conversation arising naturally rather than being mandatory.
