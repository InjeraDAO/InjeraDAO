## What is injera? — educational
If you've never eaten injera, imagine a soft, slightly tangy fermented flatbread that also helps you scoop up the food on your plate. It's at the heart of many Ethiopian and Eritrean meals.

We're building InjeraDAO around that simple power of sharing food.

Learn more: https://injera.network

## What is a DAO? — educational
DAO = decentralized autonomous organization. It's an idea about communities coordinating together.

## Press-ready paragraph
InjeraDAO is a food-first builder community centered on injera and the cultural traditions of Ethiopian and Eritrean cuisine.
